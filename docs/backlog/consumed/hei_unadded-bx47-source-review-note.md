# BX47 source review boundaries

Date: 2026-08-05  
Status: reviewed support note

## Scope

BX47 uses current first-party operating surfaces together with Dubai Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority public-register and enforcement material.

## Yhego

VARA identifies YHEGO Virtual Assets Exchange Service L.L.C, with Yhego Technology Investment L.L.C in the operator name, as an active VASP under reference `VL/26/06/001`. The licence covers exchange services for institutional, qualified, and retail investors and broker-dealer services for institutional and qualified investors.

The official domain returned an access-control response during review. That response is not treated as evidence of a dead domain or inactive platform. The URL is therefore `live_unverified`.

## CoinMENA

The current first-party site exposes local-currency funding, spot and limit orders, transfers, deposits, withdrawals, and OTC trading. The first-party about material identifies the Bahrain-headquartered CoinMENA B.S.C. (c) and the UAE entity CoinMENA FZE.

VARA lists CoinMENA FZE as active under reference `VL/23/09/001`. VARA's 2026-06-22 notice of fines identifies AML programme control failures, a financial penalty, cooperation, and a remediation plan. The notice does not state that the licence was suspended or withdrawn, so the event has no entity-status effect.

## Fasset

The current first-party site presents an operating app and account surface with digital-asset investment, payments, banking, commodities, stocks, and institutional OTC or API services.

VARA lists Fasset FZE as active under reference `VL/23/07/002` with broker-dealer services permitted for institutional, qualified, and retail investors. The record does not convert the licence into a broader exchange-services authorisation or cross-jurisdiction approval claim.

## MidChains

The current first-party site presents regulated OTC trade execution, funding, settlement, supported digital assets, and account access for institutional, corporate, family-office, and private clients. It describes the business as Emirati founded and operated from the UAE.

VARA lists Midchains FZE as active under reference `VL/25/04/008` with broker-dealer services permitted for institutional, qualified, and retail investors.

## Exclusions

BX47 does not infer:

- exact platform launch dates from licence issue dates;
- trading volume, liquidity, solvency, custody quality, or security quality;
- regulatory approval outside the cited jurisdiction;
- predecessor, successor, merger, acquisition, or rebrand relationships;
- terminal or limited status from access-control responses;
- licence suspension or shutdown from CoinMENA's financial penalty.
