# HEI Aban Tether review — 2026-08-18

## Scope

Lane A reviewed addition of Aban Tether as an exchange entity.

## Evidence reviewed

- Current first-party Aban Tether platform at `https://abantether.com/`.
- The first-party site states that the platform began in 1397 (2018), reports more than four million users and more than 1,000 tradable cryptoassets, and exposes registration/KYC, rial and crypto deposits, trading, custody and withdrawals.

## Decision

- Add Aban Tether as an `active` Iranian CEX.
- Use `2018-01-01` only as a year-level launch marker; no exact launch day is asserted.
- Do not add a sanctions/regulatory event from secondary reporting alone. A separate event requires recoverable regulator/primary evidence under the normal evidence policy.

## Safety

This change uses the normal reviewed canonical PR path. No monitoring candidate or secondary report is allowed to publish directly into canonical data.
